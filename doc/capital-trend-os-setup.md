# Capital Trend OS — Paperclip Setup Guide

This document contains the initial Paperclip setup for **Capital Trend OS**, an AI-agent-powered trend intelligence, media, research, and consulting company.

The setup is designed to help operate an AI company that monitors daily market changes and converts them into monetizable content, reports, digital products, B2B briefings, and consulting packages.

---

## 1. Company Profile

### Company Name

```text
Capital Trend OS
```

### Company Type

```text
AI-powered trend intelligence, media, research, and consulting company
```

### Company Mission

```text
Build an AI-powered trend intelligence company that helps individuals and businesses understand and monetize the shift from labor income to capital income.
```

### Target Market

```text
Individuals, solo entrepreneurs, investors, creators, HRD professionals, corporate strategy teams, DX teams, and business leaders who want to understand and monetize the shift from labor income to capital income.
```

### Core Business Model

```text
Free trend content is used for audience building and demand testing. Main revenue comes from paid reports, paid newsletter subscriptions, ebooks, online courses, B2B executive briefings, corporate trend reports, AI automation consulting, and AI agent implementation projects.
```

### Primary Revenue Sources

1. Paid trend reports
2. Paid newsletter subscriptions
3. Ebooks and digital guides
4. Online courses
5. B2B executive briefings
6. Corporate trend reports
7. AI automation consulting
8. AI agent implementation projects
9. Sponsorships and affiliate revenue
10. Long-term corporate subscriptions

### Business Principle

```text
Content is not the final product. Content is a demand-sensing mechanism, trust-building channel, and lead-generation engine. The company must prioritize high-margin digital products and B2B services over advertising revenue.
```

## 2. Main Company Goal

### Goal Title

```text
Build Capital Trend OS
```

### Main Goal

```text
Build an AI-powered trend intelligence company that monitors daily changes in labor income, capital income, consumer behavior, AI automation, investment themes, senior economy, platform work, and emerging business models.

Convert these trend signals into monetizable products such as short-form videos, blog posts, newsletters, premium reports, ebooks, online courses, B2B briefings, and consulting packages.
```

## 3. Sub Goals

### Sub Goal 1 — Daily Trend Monitoring System

```text
Build a daily system that collects and analyzes global trend signals from Korea, the United States, Japan, China, and other major markets.

Focus areas include AI automation, capital income, consumer behavior, platform work, senior economy, investment themes, new business models, and emerging creator economy trends.
```

### Sub Goal 2 — Trend Scoring and Opportunity Selection

```text
Score each trend by monetization potential, Korean market fit, content virality, B2B value, repeatability, urgency, competition level, regulatory risk, and productization potential.

Only trends with strong revenue potential and clear audience demand should move into content production or product development.
```

### Sub Goal 3 — Daily Content Production Engine

```text
Create a daily content engine that produces short-form video scripts, blog posts, newsletters, and social media posts from validated trend signals.

Content should be used to test market demand, build trust, grow audience, and generate leads for paid products.
```

### Sub Goal 4 — Premium Report and Ebook Pipeline

```text
Convert high-performing content topics into premium PDF reports, ebooks, paid newsletters, and educational materials.

The goal is to build a library of high-margin digital products based on validated market interest.
```

### Sub Goal 5 — B2B Briefing and Consulting Business

```text
Develop B2B products such as executive trend briefings, corporate trend reports, AI automation consulting, and AI agent implementation packages.

Prioritize enterprise and professional customers because B2B revenue has higher margin and faster profit potential than advertising revenue.
```

### Sub Goal 6 — AI Agent Operating Model

```text
Use AI agents to operate the company’s core workflow: trend scouting, market analysis, content planning, script writing, blog writing, report drafting, fact checking, compliance review, monetization planning, and performance analysis.

Human leadership must approve final strategy, sensitive claims, paid products, and external publishing.
```

### Sub Goal 7 — Monetization System

```text
Build multiple revenue streams including paid reports, subscriptions, ebooks, online courses, B2B briefings, consulting projects, affiliate revenue, sponsorships, and eventually AI agent system implementation services.

Advertising revenue should be treated as secondary, not as the main business model.
```

### Sub Goal 8 — Performance Feedback Loop

```text
Track daily performance across YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, blog, newsletter, and paid product sales.

Use performance data to identify which topics should be expanded into reports, ebooks, courses, consulting offers, or B2B packages.
```

### Sub Goal 9 — 12-Month Financial Milestones

```text
Month 1: Build the monitoring and content production system. Publish at least 50 short-form content pieces and 10 blog/newsletter posts.

Month 3: Launch the first paid report or ebook and acquire first paying customers.

Month 6: Reach KRW 10–20 million in monthly revenue.

Month 12: Reach KRW 100 million in monthly revenue and KRW 40 million in monthly operating profit.
```

### Sub Goal 10 — Quality and Trust Standards

```text
Maintain a high editorial standard. Avoid low-quality AI spam, fake expertise, exaggerated investment claims, copied content, and misleading synthetic media.

Every paid product must include original analysis, practical business insight, and clear value for the target customer.
```

## 8. Trend Scoring Framework

Score each trend out of 100.

| Category | Weight | Description |
|---|---:|---|
| Monetization potential | 20 | Can this become paid content, consulting, or a product? |
| Korean market fit | 15 | Can this be applied to Korea? |
| B2B value | 15 | Would a company pay to understand or implement this? |
| Content virality | 10 | Can this become strong short-form content? |
| Search demand | 10 | Are people actively searching for this? |
| Repeatability | 10 | Can this become a recurring topic or product line? |
| Urgency | 5 | Is the timing important now? |
| Competition level | 5 | Is the market not too saturated? |
| Regulatory/platform risk | 5 | Is the risk manageable? |
| Productization potential | 5 | Can this become a report, ebook, course, or service? |

### Decision Rules

```text
80–100: Priority topic. Create content and product angle immediately.
70–79: Strong topic. Create short-form and blog/newsletter content.
60–69: Watchlist. Monitor further before productizing.
Below 60: Ignore unless there is a strategic reason.
```

### Productization Rules

```text
High virality + medium monetization = short-form video
High search demand + medium monetization = blog/newsletter
High monetization + high repeatability = paid report or ebook
High B2B value + high urgency = executive briefing or consulting offer
High Korean market fit + overseas case = localization business analysis
```

## 14. Operating Philosophy

```text
Do not build a content farm.
Build a market-sensing and monetization system.

Do not chase views only.
Use views to find demand.

Do not depend on advertising revenue.
Use content to sell reports, briefings, consulting, and implementation projects.

Do not let AI publish without human judgment.
Use AI for speed, but keep human responsibility for trust and strategy.
```
